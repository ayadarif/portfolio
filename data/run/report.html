Themes

App stability

  • App won't open after latest update (28): Customers report the app refuses to open, shows a white screen or spins forever, often right after an update or reinstall.
  • Crashes or exits during transactions (22): App closes, logs out or hangs mid-transaction, such as at PIN entry, payment, transfer or description entry.
  • Network errors and no connection in app (22): App reports poor, weak or unavailable network, blocking login, registration or account access while other apps work.
  • Slow and laggy app performance (17): Customers find the app slow to load, lagging or delayed, often saying a recent version is slower than before.
  • Update problems and lost data (11): After updating, accounts or beneficiaries vanish, updates loop or are unavailable, or version notices conflict with the store.
  • Device-specific, install and setup failures (8): App cannot be downloaded, installed or set up on certain phones, or fails at PIN or consent setup.

Login and access

  • Login fails after update or with errors (21): Customers cannot log in, especially after an update, due to crashes, blank screens, errors, slow loading or rejected credentials.
  • Cannot create or use transaction PIN (19): Customers fail to create, set, reset or use a transaction/mobile PIN, with errors, invalid-PIN messages or missing forgot-PIN options.
  • New phone: OTP or device activation fails (16): After changing phones, customers cannot activate or re-register the device because OTP is not sent, card verification fails or the device is not found.
  • Registration and sign-up fails (15): New users cannot register or create an account due to crashes, network errors, account-exists or account-number errors, or card requirements.
  • Device limit or device already exists (9): Activation or login is blocked by maximum device limit, device-exists messages, or repeated activation prompts.
  • Rooted, jailbroken or developer-mode blocks (5): The app refuses to run, claiming the device is rooted, jailbroken, an emulator or has USB debugging enabled.

Transfers and payments

  • Other-bank transfers fail or blank after update (13): Transfers to other banks fail, show blank lists or endless blank pages, especially since the recent app update.
  • App crashes, hangs or logs out during transactions (12): The app freezes, exits, logs out or will not open when customers start or authorise a transaction, such as at PIN entry.
  • Debited but transfer failed or not credited (10): Money is deducted from the sender for failed or pending transactions, with no credit to the recipient and slow or missing reversals.
  • Debit account selector blank, blocking transfers (8): Customers see an empty debit account field or blank page when starting a transaction, so they cannot pick an account or transfer.
  • Transfer and daily limit confusion or reduction (5): Customers hit unexplained transfer limits, a reduced 1 million daily cap, or a limit upgrade option that is unavailable.
  • Beneficiaries missing or cannot be confirmed (2): Saved beneficiaries do not show, and adding or confirming beneficiaries fails or restarts the flow.

Money and balance

  • Failed transfers debited but not reversed (27): Transfers, purchases or bill payments fail or go undelivered yet the sender is debited without timely reversal.
  • Transaction history missing or limited (12): Customers cannot view, scroll or filter past transactions, with history capped or disappearing.
  • Balance not visible after app update (11): After the new version, balance or accounts are blank or missing, blocking transactions.
  • Unauthorized or unexplained debits and charges (11): Customers report money removed without their knowledge, unexpected charges or repeated deductions.
  • Incoming money not credited (5): Transfers or disbursements sent to the customer have not arrived in their account.
  • Receipts wrong, missing or undownloadable (3): Receipts cannot be downloaded or show wrong dates, and old receipts are hard to get.

Design and usability

  • Dislike new interface, want old version (17): Customers find the redesigned app worse, complex or not user friendly and ask for a fix or the previous version.
  • Transfers and transactions fail after update (14): Since the update, customers cannot complete transfers, other-bank transfers or payments, or the transfer screen fails to open.
  • Slow, crashing or won't open or log in (14): The app lags, crashes, shows blank screens or errors, or fails to open or log in after updates.
  • Update prompts, T&C and login friction (9): Repeated terms prompts, extra OTP steps, device or card login limits and confusing update notices annoy customers.
  • Receipts and history missing or incomplete (8): Receipts cannot be downloaded, are inaccurate or are unavailable for past transfers, and history is limited or lacks detail.
  • Account balance or accounts not displayed (6): After updating, balances are unavailable or accounts and debit selectors show blank or wrong, blocking transactions.

Method

  • Dataset: Bank-Review (Google Play reviews of Nigerian banks' apps), Federal University Lokoja
  • Dataset url: https://huggingface.co/datasets/Federal-University-Lokoja/Bank-Review
  • Dataset licence: Apache-2.0 (per the dataset card, checked 2026-11-24)
  • Dataset note: Reviews were collected by the dataset's authors, not by this project.
  • Sample: 1,000 reviews, 2023, UBA, Access Bank and First Bank of Nigeria, seed 20261124; 100 held out for the accuracy check
  • Model: claude-sonnet-5-5; Claude Code 2.1.331 (Claude Code); prompt hash 3dd5d5f6ac2d62ad
  • Not classified: 0 reviews

Accuracy check against 100 hand-labelled reviews: the model's first category matched one of the labeller's categories on 91 of 100 (95% interval 83.8% to 95.2%); Cohen's kappa 0.84; keyword baseline 58 of 100.
